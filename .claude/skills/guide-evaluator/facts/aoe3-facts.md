**Scope**: Age of Empires III, original (2005) plus the Definitive Edition (2020) which is the
version played today. Facts specific to one or the other are marked.

## Ages

- AoE3 has **five Ages**, one more than AoE1/AoE2/AoE4: **Discovery → Colonial → Fortress →
  Industrial → Imperial**. Discovery is exploration and economy; Colonial unlocks early military;
  Fortress adds fortification and the full military; Industrial brings a strong economy with
  factories and artillery; Imperial unlocks all buildings and upgrades.
  - source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Age_of_Empires_III
- "All Ages cost food and coin to advance to, except the Colonial Age, which only costs food
  (800)." - source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Age_of_Empires_III
- Age-up also runs through a **Politician system**: choosing a politician grants a bonus on
  advancing. There is no equivalent in AoE1/AoE2/AoE4.
  - source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Age_of_Empires_III
  ⚠️ CẦN NGƯỜI DUYỆT - Wikipedia names the Politician system and its age-advancement bonus role
  but does not enumerate the politicians or their bonuses. Do not list specifics.

## Home City and shipment cards - the signature mechanic

- The **Home City** is "a second city, a powerhouse that is separated from the active game. It
  cannot be attacked or destroyed." This is the mechanic that most defines AoE3 against the rest
  of the series. - source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Age_of_Empires_III
- Players earn **experience points** by constructing buildings, training units, killing enemies
  and collecting treasures. Crossing XP thresholds grants **shipments** from the Home City.
  - source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Age_of_Empires_III
- The Home City has **five buildings** the player picks cards and customizations from: the New
  World Trading Company, the Military Academy, the Cathedral, the Manufacturing Plant, and the
  Harbor. - source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Age_of_Empires_III
- A deck holds **twenty cards**, with extra slots earned every 10 Home City levels.
  - source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Age_of_Empires_III
- Wikipedia describes the design as combining "real-time strategy and role-playing features" -
  meta-progression that persists between matches, which AoE1/AoE2/AoE4 do not have.
  - source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Age_of_Empires_III

## Military: the gunpowder era

- AoE3 is a **gunpowder-era game**: units include muskets, rifles, cannons and mortars. "Artillery
  classes also make use of ranged weapons, primarily cannon and mortars; however, there is also
  artillery armed with grenades." - source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Age_of_Empires_III
- ⚠️ CẦN NGƯỜI DUYỆT - AoE3's counter system (the musketeer/cavalry/skirmisher triangle the

## Natives, mercenaries, trade

- **Twelve Native American tribes** are available as allies, not as playable factions. A player
  allies by building a trading post at their camp, unlocking unique units and improvements.
  - source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Age_of_Empires_III
- **Mercenaries** work differently: shipped from the Home City or hired from saloons, "for a lot
  of gold". - source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Age_of_Empires_III

## Definitive Edition: release, civs, support

- AoE3:DE released **15 October 2020**, adding 4K graphics, rebuilt 3D assets, improved
  destruction animations, new modes (Art of War, Historical Battles, Diplomacy, Tycoon), an
  "extreme" AI difficulty, spectator mode, mod support and cross-network multiplayer. It shipped
  with two new civilizations, **Incas and Swedes**.
  - source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Age_of_Empires_III:_Definitive_Edition ,
    https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Age_of_Empires_III
- Post-launch civilizations: **United States** (13 Apr 2021), **Ethiopians and Hausa** (2 Aug
  2021, The African Royals), **Mexico** (1 Dec 2021), **Italians and Maltese** (26 May 2022,
  Knights of the Mediterranean).
  - source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Age_of_Empires_III:_Definitive_Edition
- **The Baltic Powers (Danes, Poles) is dated 10 September 2026, which is in the FUTURE as of
  2026-08-08.** Treat it as announced, not shipped. Any article written before that date must not
  describe it in the past tense.
  - source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Age_of_Empires_III:_Definitive_Edition
- **Do not print a total civilization count for AoE3 either.** The same drift that makes the AoE2
  count unreliable applies here: base roster plus six expansions across six years. Describe the
  shape instead.

## Series pacing

- The AoE4 store records a community-accepted cross-title pacing ranking, confirmed by the owner
  on 2026-07-06: **AoE3 > AoE1 > AoE4 > AoE2**, fastest to slowest. AoE3 is the fastest game in
  the series by that ranking. It is not an official benchmark but is treated as true for this
  audience and must not be flagged as an error.
  - source: `.claude/skills/guide-evaluator/facts/aoe4-facts.md`, "Cross-title speed ranking"
